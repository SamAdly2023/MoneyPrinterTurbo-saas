Data deletion

How to delete your Vidzy data, including data from connected Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn and Rumble accounts.

Option 1 - disconnect a platform

In Vidzy, open Settings, find the connected platform and click Disconnect. This deletes the access token we stored for that platform straight away. For Facebook and Instagram you can also remove Vidzy on Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations.

Option 2 - delete your whole account and data

  1. Email samadly728@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Vidzy data.
  2. We confirm the request and permanently delete your account, profile, videos, scripts, stream settings and every stored platform token within 30 days (usually within a few days).
  3. We email you when it is done.
Some records may be kept where the law requires it, for example payment records held for tax purposes. Videos you already published on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or Rumble stay on those platforms until you remove them there.
